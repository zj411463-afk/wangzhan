Quick answer

What does 67 mean?

“67” (said “six seven”) is a viral, intentionally vague meme. People often say it as a playful response with an up-and-down, palms-up hand gesture—roughly suggesting “so-so,” “maybe this, maybe that,” or simply joining the joke.

The phrase became popular through the song Doot Doot (6 7) by Skrilla, basketball edits, and the viral “67 Kid.” It does not have one fixed dictionary definition, and its nonsense is part of the appeal.

Good to know: There is no official Unicode “67 emoji.” The options above are community-made combinations of numbers, gestures, and reaction emojis.

67 emoji FAQ

Is 67 an official emoji?

No. Unicode has digit keycaps such as 6️⃣ and 7️⃣, but “67” is not a single official emoji. People combine characters to represent the meme.

Why is 67 associated with basketball?

Basketball edits helped spread the sound and phrase, so 🏀 often appears in 67 combinations. The meme later expanded far beyond basketball.

Is 67 inappropriate?

The meme is usually used as harmless internet slang. Context still matters, but the number itself is not inherently offensive.
