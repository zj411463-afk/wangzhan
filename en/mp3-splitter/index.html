MP3 Splitter Online: Split MP3 by Time Without Re-encoding

Divide one MP3 into several files without uploading or re-encoding it. Choose a fixed duration, or enter your own cut points. For one excerpt instead of several parts, use the audio cutter.

Local processing · No audio upload

MP3

Choose an MP3 to begin.

Split mode

Seconds or mm:ss, separated by commas. Example: 0:30, 1:00

Parts

    Up to 256 MB and 500 parts. ZIP creation needs additional device memory.

    MP3 cuts align to frames, not exact samples. Some files use a bit reservoir: a part's opening may be shortened or affected while the decoder initializes. Preview the exported parts. Original Xing/seek metadata is omitted to avoid stale whole-file durations.

    Split by duration or by timestamps

    For a long recording, enter 300 seconds to create roughly five-minute parts; the final part may be shorter. For chapter boundaries, switch to timestamps and enter 0:30, 1:00. Cut points are sorted and aligned to nearby MP3 frames. The result list shows the actual boundaries before downloading.

    What does no re-encoding mean?

    The splitter copies existing audio frames; it does not change bitrate, sample rate or channels, and it cannot restore detail already lost in the MP3. Frame boundaries and decoder dependencies mean this is not sample-accurate or guaranteed gapless editing. Listen to the beginning and end of each exported part.

    Download on a phone or computer

    Preview any exported part, then download it individually or choose one ZIP containing all parts. A browser may save directly to Downloads instead of opening a dialog. Files remain on your device; memory, supported playback and ZIP size depend on your browser. Conversion, merging and fades are not included.
